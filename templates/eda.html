Plot Descriptions
Distribution of Class Levels

Bar plot shows count of each class level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) to understand student distribution, aiding model stratification by class.

Performance Score vs Heart Rate

Box plot compares heart rate ranges to performance scores, revealing physiological impact on educational outcomes.

Performance Score vs Pitch Accuracy

Box plot assesses pitch accuracy ranges against performance, identifying accuracy's role in musical education success.

Engagement Score vs Focus Time

Correlation Heatmap
